Data Deletion

Developer: Afterhours
Last updated: September 30, 2026

Local data and online data are separate Clearing IponQuest app storage or uninstalling removes local data from that installation, but it does not request deletion of an online account, linked Personal Cloud data, Household resources, exported files, or a Google Play subscription.

Delete individual records inside IponQuest

Use the app's supported edit and delete actions to remove individual finance records where those actions are available. After deletion, confirm that the relevant ledger, balances, reports, goals, recurring state, or other dependent views have updated as expected.

Delete all local IponQuest data

Android menu names vary by device. A typical path is:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Open Apps or Applications.
  3. Select IponQuest.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Choose Clear storage, Clear data, or the equivalent option.
  6. Confirm the Android warning.

This removes supported local IponQuest information from that installation, including local finance data, settings, progression, cosmetics, and device-local security state.

If important local data has not been exported or backed up, clearing storage can be irreversible.

Delete by uninstalling

Uninstalling IponQuest removes local app data associated with that installation, subject to Android/device policy. It does not perform online account deletion and does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Delete an online IponQuest account and Personal Cloud data

When account/cloud features are enabled, use IponQuest's account-deletion action while signed in. Account deletion is separate from sign-out and requires recent authentication.

The deletion process is designed to remove the account's owned Personal Cloud dataset and Supabase Auth identity after required cleanup. If cleanup or identity deletion cannot be confirmed, the process may pause and require retry rather than reporting a false completion.

Online account deletion preserves local finance data on the device. If you also want the local copy removed, clear app storage or uninstall after confirming any data you want to keep has been exported safely.

Household owners and members

If you are a non-owner Household member, account deletion can detach your membership while preserving the Household's shared resources for the remaining authorized members.

If you own a Household, you must first transfer Household ownership or explicitly delete the Household before personal account deletion can finish. Household deletion is separate from personal-account deletion and removes Household-owned collaboration resources such as memberships, invitations, shared budgets, and shared goals.

Google Play subscriptions are separate

Deleting an IponQuest account, clearing local data, or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.

Delete exported files separately

Clearing app storage, uninstalling IponQuest, or deleting an online account does not automatically delete files that you previously exported, saved, copied, or shared.

Review and delete copies from every relevant destination, which may include:

  • Downloads, Documents, or another folder on the device
  • Cloud-drive folders selected by the user
  • Email attachments or messaging apps
  • External storage or another device
  • CSV, PDF, ZIP, or Backup V3 JSON copies retained elsewhere
  • Older V1 or V2 backup files that you kept for import

Receipt images and OCR drafts

Receipt images and raw OCR text are processed on the device for scanning. If you independently saved, copied, photographed, or shared a receipt outside IponQuest, delete those copies separately from their destination.

Notifications and Android system records

Turning off recurring-bill reminders in IponQuest stops supported future reminder scheduling. Android may separately retain notification history according to device settings. Manage that history through Android settings where available.

Request deletion help outside the app

If you cannot use the in-app account-deletion flow, contact iponquest@gmail.com and state that you need IponQuest account-deletion help. Do not attach financial exports, receipt images, passwords, PINs, authentication tokens, purchase tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

Account ownership must be verified before account-specific assistance can be provided. Some deletion steps may still require recent authentication or Household-owner action. Afterhours cannot remotely erase finance data that exists only on your device.

Deletion timing and technical state

IponQuest does not promise an exact deletion-completion or server-retention period on this page. Account deletion is designed to fail safely and remain retryable when required cleanup cannot be confirmed instead of falsely reporting completion.
