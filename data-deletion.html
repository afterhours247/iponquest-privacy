IponQuest

Data Deletion

Developer: NiceTry
Last updated: July 20, 2026

No online IponQuest account The current V1 app does not create an online account and NiceTry does not store your budgeting data on a NiceTry server. There is therefore no remote account record to request deletion of.

Delete individual records inside IponQuest

Use the app’s supported edit and delete actions to remove individual transactions or recurring bills. After deletion, confirm that the relevant ledger, totals, reports, and recurring-bill state have updated as expected.

Delete all local IponQuest data

Android menu names vary by device. A typical path is:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Open Apps or Applications.
  3. Select IponQuest.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Choose Clear storage, Clear data, or the equivalent option.
  6. Confirm the Android warning.

This removes supported local IponQuest information from that installation, including transactions, recurring bills, budget settings, preferences, progression, earned coins, badges, names, and cosmetic ownership or equipment state.

Delete by uninstalling

Uninstalling IponQuest removes local app data associated with that installation. Android behavior may vary by device and managed-device policy.

Delete exported files separately

Clearing app storage or uninstalling IponQuest does not automatically delete files that you previously exported, saved, copied, or shared.

Review and delete copies from every relevant destination, which may include:

  • Downloads, Documents, or another folder on the device
  • Cloud-drive folders selected by the user
  • Email attachments or messaging apps
  • External storage or another device
  • CSV, PDF, ZIP, or Backup V2 JSON copies retained elsewhere

Notifications and Android system records

Turning off recurring-bill reminders in IponQuest stops supported future reminder scheduling. Android may separately retain notification history according to the device’s settings. Manage that history through Android settings where available.

Need help?

For questions about deleting local IponQuest data, contact iponquest@gmail.com. Do not email exported backup files, transaction lists, bank details, passwords, or other sensitive financial information.

Because NiceTry does not receive the current app’s local budgeting data, support cannot remotely inspect or erase data stored only on your device.
