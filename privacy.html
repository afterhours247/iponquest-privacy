Privacy Policy

Developer: Afterhours
Effective date: September 30, 2026
Last updated: September 30, 2026

Local-first, with optional online features IponQuest keeps its working personal-finance data on the device. Core manual budgeting can be used without an account. Signing in alone does not link, upload, merge, or transfer ownership of the local finance dataset.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how IponQuest handles information in the Android app. IponQuest is a local-first budget tracker for recording income and expenses, reviewing reports, managing accounts and recurring items, setting budgets and goals, and optionally using RPG-style progression and cosmetic features.

Some IponQuest builds may also enable optional Google sign-in, Personal Cloud Sync, Household collaboration, Receipt Quick Add authorization, Play Integrity security checks, and Google Play Plus subscriptions. Availability depends on the app build and service configuration.

This policy does not govern third-party apps or services that a user independently chooses as a destination for exported or shared files.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features used, IponQuest stores user-created and app-generated information locally, including:

Finance and planning information

  • Income and expense transactions, dates, amounts, categories, notes, and related record identifiers
  • Accounts or wallets, balances, reconciliation information, and internal transfers
  • Monthly spending limits, savings goals, category limits, custom categories, and currency/settings state
  • Recurring definitions, occurrence/payment state, due information, and related notes
  • Goal Pots and related local planning information

Preferences, profile, progression, and cosmetics

  • Adventure or Classic mode, appearance, reminder, onboarding, and accessibility preferences
  • Adventurer/profile naming information entered by the user
  • Level, XP, coins, streaks, quest/progression state, badges, avatars, pets, Shop ownership, temporary cosmetic passes, and equipped cosmetic/Guild Hall choices
  • Home customization and other supported presentation preferences

Local security state

If App Lock is enabled, IponQuest stores local security state needed to verify the configured PIN using Android Keystore-backed protection. Raw PIN values are not stored in normal app data, and App Lock credentials are not part of Backup V3 or Personal Cloud Sync.

3. Optional account sign-in

IponQuest can support optional Google sign-in through Supabase Auth. The authentication flow may process your Google account identifier and profile information such as display name, email address, and profile photo, together with authentication/session information required to keep you signed in and authorize protected requests.

Authentication, local dataset ownership, and cloud-sync authorization are separate. Signing in does not automatically upload, download, merge, restore, or claim ownership of the finance data already stored on your device.

Signing out keeps local finance data on the device. Switching accounts does not silently transfer ownership or merge one account's linked dataset into another account.

4. Personal Cloud Sync

Personal Cloud Sync is optional. It begins only after the user signs in and explicitly links the active local dataset to the account.

When linked, supported finance records may be processed through Supabase and synchronized in the background while the account is signed in and network conditions allow it. The supported Personal Cloud contract includes accounts, transactions, transfers, recurring items and occurrences, and Goal Pots, including associated record identifiers and supported notes or fields.

Local settings, RPG progression, and cosmetic choices are outside the Personal Cloud finance-record contract. Pending changes may be queued locally and replayed after connectivity returns, but they must remain bound to the correct linked account and dataset.

5. Household collaboration

Household is an optional online collaboration feature. Supabase may process Household identifiers, invitations, membership/role information, and approved shared planning data such as shared budgets and shared goals.

A member's personal finance dataset is not automatically copied into Household space and is not shared with other Household members. Access to Household data depends on server-authoritative membership and role permissions.

6. Receipt Quick Add

Receipt images and raw OCR text are processed on the device using Google ML Kit text recognition. The receipt image and raw OCR text are not uploaded by the scanning path for server-side OCR.

Receipt scanning still uses online authorization. Supabase may process account identity, an opaque scan-attempt identifier, quota/completion records, and security or anti-abuse information. Play Integrity may also be used to verify the app/device request.

The extracted draft must be reviewed and can be corrected before a transaction is saved. If Personal Cloud Sync is linked, the saved transaction fields may later sync under the normal Personal Cloud contract; this does not upload the receipt image or raw OCR text.

7. Billing, Plus, and Play Integrity

When paid Plus features are offered and used, Google Play handles the purchase transaction. IponQuest sends purchase-verification information, including the purchase token and product information, to the trusted backend so the purchase can be verified with Google Play. A local Play purchase result alone does not grant Plus.

IponQuest stores entitlement-status information needed to present verified access. It does not store payment-card details.

Protected online features may use Google Play Integrity. Google and the IponQuest backend may process app/device integrity proofs and request/security metadata to check app authenticity, bind requests, prevent replay, and reduce abuse.

8. Advertising, analytics, and crash reporting

The current IponQuest candidate source does not directly include an in-app advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or dedicated crash-reporting SDK. Platform services such as Google Play may independently collect diagnostic or store/service information under their own policies.

9. User-controlled exports and Backup V3

IponQuest creates export or backup files only when the user chooses to export, save, or share them through Android system flows.

  • CSV: may contain transaction information.
  • PDF and ZIP: may contain budget, transaction, report, and derived summary information.
  • Backup V3 JSON: may contain supported local finance, settings, profile/progression, and cosmetic state.

Backup V3 excludes account credentials, account/cloud ownership authority, Household authority, sync operational state, and temporary workflow/security state. Older V1 and V2 backup files remain supported import formats.

Backup JSON and other exported files are not password-encrypted by IponQuest. They may contain sensitive financial or personal information. After a file is saved or shared outside IponQuest, the selected destination controls that copy and may have its own privacy and security practices.

10. Notifications

IponQuest may request Android notification permission for optional recurring-bill reminders. A reminder may display the bill name and amount. Android and the user's notification settings control notification delivery and lock-screen visibility.

Reminders are best-effort local notifications. They do not make payments or automatically create transactions.

11. Storage, transmission, and Android backup

IponQuest uses local Android storage such as Room and DataStore for its working state. Android operating-system cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for IponQuest app data in the current release configuration. User-controlled export/restore is separate from Android operating-system backup.

Optional network features use the configured Supabase and Google/Google Play services described above. Ordinary local budgeting does not synchronize personal finance merely because the app is installed, opened, or used offline.

Opening this published policy is an explicit user action that sends the public HTTPS page request to the browser/site infrastructure. It does not upload the local IponQuest finance database.

12. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the device until it is edited, deleted, cleared, or removed by the user. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data from that installation but does not request deletion of an online IponQuest account or linked cloud records.

Online account deletion is a separate authenticated process. It requires recent sign-in and is designed to remove the account's Personal Cloud dataset and Supabase Auth identity after required server-side cleanup. The process may pause or require retry if cleanup cannot be confirmed.

If the user is a Household member, account deletion can detach that membership while preserving shared Household resources. A Household owner must transfer ownership or explicitly delete the Household before personal account deletion can finish. Explicit Household deletion removes the Household-owned collaboration resources.

Deleting an IponQuest account does not automatically erase local finance data from the device and does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Google Play subscription management is separate.

Limited server-owned deletion and recovery evidence is intentionally designed to outlive the removed Supabase Auth user so interrupted deletion can be reconciled and a later account generation cannot silently reclaim deleted data. Current source includes the deleted account identifier/generation, a one-way hash of the recovery capability, deletion status and cleanup state, retry/status timestamps, and a deletion-generation barrier. These records are not the deleted Personal Cloud finance dataset and are not exposed for normal client access. The current source does not define a fixed retention period for this technical deletion evidence.

13. Security

IponQuest uses Android local storage, Android system file flows, authentication/session controls for online features, server authorization, and Play Integrity for applicable protected requests. No storage or network system is perfectly secure.

Users should protect their device, lock screen, Google account, IponQuest App Lock when enabled, and exported files. Do not share authentication tokens, purchase tokens, backup files, or other sensitive information with support.

14. Children

IponQuest is a real budgeting utility. It contains optional RPG-style visuals and progression, but it is not designed as a children's game.

15. Financial guidance

Safe to Spend, Daily Spend Limit, reports, insights, forecasts, and related values are estimates based on information entered into IponQuest. They are not financial, tax, investment, legal, or professional advice and are not guarantees.

16. Changes to this policy

Afterhours may update this policy when IponQuest changes its permissions, local storage, account features, cloud or Household services, exports, notifications, receipt processing, dependencies, advertising, analytics, billing, security/attestation, or other data handling. The current version will show an updated date.
