IponQuest

Privacy Policy

Developer: NiceTry
Effective date: July 20, 2026
Last updated: July 20, 2026

Local-first by design IponQuest currently has no IponQuest account system, NiceTry-operated cloud sync, bank connection, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, real-money billing, or backend service.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the current Android version of IponQuest handles information. IponQuest is a local budget tracker that lets users manually record income and expenses, review reports, manage recurring bills, set spending and savings goals, and optionally use lightweight RPG-style progression and cosmetic customization.

This policy applies to the IponQuest app published by NiceTry. It does not govern third-party apps or services that a user chooses as a destination for exported or shared files.

2. Information stored on your device

IponQuest stores user-created and app-generated information locally on the Android device. Depending on the features used, this may include:

Budget and transaction information

  • Income and expense transactions
  • Transaction categories, dates, amounts, and notes
  • Monthly spending limit and savings goal
  • Category spending limits and custom categories
  • Recurring bill names, categories, amounts, due days, notes, active state, and payment-related state

Preferences and profile information

  • Adventure or Classic display mode
  • Reminder preferences
  • Onboarding completion state
  • Accessibility text-size preference
  • Adventurer name entered by the user

Progression and cosmetic information

  • Level, XP, coins, streaks, daily-quest claim state, and related progress
  • Unlocked and equipped badge preferences
  • Shop ownership and temporary cosmetic-pass expiry information
  • Equipped Guild Hall weather, background, and supported naming state

These items are local app data. They are not used to create an online IponQuest account.

3. How the app uses local information

IponQuest uses the information entered or generated in the app to provide budgeting and app functionality, including:

  • Calculating budget left, saved potential, overspent states, and safe-to-spend estimates
  • Displaying ledger, report, trend, and category summaries
  • Tracking recurring-bill status and reminder eligibility
  • Maintaining local settings, progression, earned coins, badges, and cosmetic choices
  • Creating user-requested exports and Backup V2 files

4. Storage and transmission

The current app stores supported data locally using Android storage technologies such as Room and DataStore. NiceTry does not receive the user’s budgeting data on a NiceTry server because the current V1 app does not operate an account, cloud-sync, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or backend service.

Android operating-system backup and device-transfer behavior for IponQuest app data is disabled in the current release configuration. User-controlled export and restore features are separate from Android operating-system backup.

5. User-controlled exports and Backup V2

IponQuest can create files only when the user chooses to export, save, or share them through Android system flows.

  • CSV: may contain transaction information.
  • PDF and ZIP: may contain budget, transaction, report, and derived summary information.
  • Backup V2 JSON: may contain supported local app data such as transactions, recurring bills, budget settings, categories, reminder settings, progression, wallet, badges, names, and cosmetic ownership or equipment state.

Exported files may contain private financial, profile, progression, or cosmetic information. Backup V2 JSON files are not password-encrypted by IponQuest. Users should store and share exported files carefully.

After a file is saved or shared outside IponQuest, the selected destination controls that copy. The destination may have its own privacy and security practices.

6. Notifications

IponQuest may request Android notification permission for optional recurring-bill reminders. A reminder may display the bill name and amount so the user can judge urgency. Android and the user’s notification settings control notification and lock-screen visibility.

Reminders are best-effort local notifications. They do not create transactions automatically and are not exact alarms. Users can disable reminders in IponQuest or through Android settings.

7. Sharing and third-party services

NiceTry does not sell IponQuest user data. The current app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, authentication, backend, cloud-storage, or billing SDKs.

When a user deliberately exports or shares a file to another app or service, that destination is selected by the user and is outside NiceTry’s control.

8. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the device until it is edited, deleted, cleared, or removed by the user. Users can:

  • Edit or delete supported transactions and recurring bills inside IponQuest
  • Clear all local IponQuest app data through Android settings
  • Uninstall IponQuest to remove local app data from the device
  • Delete exported files separately from each destination where they were saved or shared

IponQuest currently offers no online account, so there is no separate remote account-deletion process. Detailed instructions are available on the Data Deletion page.

9. Security

IponQuest relies on Android local storage and Android system file flows. No storage method is perfectly secure. Users should protect their device, lock screen, backups, and exported files.

NiceTry does not claim that exported files are encrypted, that the device cannot be compromised, or that third-party destinations are secure.

10. Children

IponQuest is a real budgeting utility. It includes optional RPG-style visuals and progression, but it is not designed as a children’s game. NiceTry does not knowingly operate an account system or service that collects children’s personal information in the current V1 app.

11. Financial guidance

Safe to Spend, Daily Spend Limit, reports, insights, and related values are estimates based on information entered into IponQuest. They are not financial, tax, investment, legal, or professional advice and are not guarantees.

12. Changes to this policy

NiceTry may update this policy when IponQuest changes its permissions, storage, exports, notifications, dependencies, network behavior, account features, cloud services, advertising, analytics, billing, or other data handling. The updated version will show a new effective or last-updated date.
