IponQuest

Support

Help with app behavior, privacy questions, exports, backups, and release-related support.

Public support address

iponquest@gmail.com

Copy this address into your preferred email app or webmail service. NiceTry cannot remotely inspect data that exists only on your device.

What to include

To make troubleshooting easier, include:

  • A short description of what you were trying to do
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • Your Android version and device model
  • Your IponQuest app version, when available
  • Clear reproduction steps
  • A screenshot only when it does not expose private information

Do not email sensitive information

Do not send:

  • Bank-account, card, payment, or government-identification details
  • Passwords, PINs, recovery codes, or authentication secrets
  • Real transaction histories or private financial reports
  • Backup V2 JSON, CSV, PDF, or ZIP files containing real data
  • Keystores, signing credentials, API keys, or Play Console secrets

Use fictional or redacted information when demonstrating an issue.

Common support topics

Local data and deletion

See the Data Deletion page for removing individual records, clearing all local app data, uninstalling, and deleting exported copies.

Exports and Backup V2

Exports are created only when the user initiates them. The selected Android destination controls the exported copy. Backup V2 JSON files are not password-encrypted by IponQuest, so keep them private.

Recurring-bill reminders

Reminders require the relevant Android permission and settings. They are best-effort local notifications, not exact alarms, and they do not make payments or create transactions automatically.

Financial calculations

IponQuest calculations depend on the data entered into the app. Verify transaction dates, amounts, categories, spending limits, savings goals, and recurring-bill details when a result looks unexpected.

Privacy questions

Read the Privacy Policy. Questions about the policy may be sent to the same public support address shown above.
