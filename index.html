Current privacy posture

Local-first, with optional online features

IponQuest keeps its working personal-finance data on the device and supports core manual budgeting without an account. Signing in does not automatically upload or claim the local finance dataset.

If you choose supported online features, IponQuest may use Supabase for account and trusted cloud operations, Google Play for purchases, Play Integrity for security checks, and ML Kit for on-device receipt text recognition. Personal Cloud requires explicit dataset linking, and Household shares only approved Household planning data with authorized members.

User-controlled CSV, PDF, ZIP, and Backup V3 JSON files may contain private information. Copies saved or shared outside IponQuest remain under the destination's control.
