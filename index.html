Current V1 privacy posture

IponQuest is an Android-first, local-first budget tracker. The current V1 app stores budgeting, recurring-bill, settings, progression, and cosmetic information on the device. It does not create an IponQuest account and does not provide bank connections, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, real-money billing, or a NiceTry-operated backend.

Users may choose to export CSV, PDF, ZIP, or Backup V2 JSON files through Android system flows. Those files can contain private information and remain under the user’s control after export.
